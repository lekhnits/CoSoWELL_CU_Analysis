My wife was finishing up washing dishes yesterday evening. she was somewhat distracted, thinking about a wedding she would be attending this coming Saturday. She was so distracted, she did not notice that the water in the kitchen sink started to overflow, spilling onto the floor. At the same time, behind her back, our son and daughter, knowing their mother was distracted, were sneaking cookies out of the cookie jar in the cupboard. Our son had to get up on a stool to reach the jar. he was off balance and the stool was about to tip over when I happened to come into the room and see what was happening. "What's going on?!" I shouted. it startled my son, who fell on his backside and snapped my wife out of her daze. Luckily my son wasn't hurt and my wife turned the water off before much water spilled onto the floor. I took a picture with my phone. We all got a good laugh out of it.
